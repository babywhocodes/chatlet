## About

Studying shouldn't start with a mountain of prep work. Chatlet skips the busywork and goes straight from idea to flashcards. There are no PDFs to upload, no notes to scan, and no hundreds of cards to type by hand. Describe a topic such as *"Spanish verb conjugations"*, *"World War II key dates"* or *"JavaScript array methods"*, and Chatlet generates a complete deck for you.

Chatlet is available on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.babywhocodes.chatlet) and is now open source.

## Features

- **Instant generation**: describe a topic and your deck appears immediately.
- **Smart question-answer pairs**: cards are structured around context, not random trivia.
- **Any subject, any level**: school exams, language learning, professional certifications, hobbies.
- **Edit and customize**: fine-tune cards, add your own, or regenerate ones that don't fit.
- **Built-in study modes**: spaced repetition, shuffle mode, and quizzes to track what you've mastered.
- **Save and organize**: keep decks organized by subject, folder, or tag.
- **Offline study**: review your flashcards anywhere.
- **Clean, distraction-free UI**: the focus stays on learning.
